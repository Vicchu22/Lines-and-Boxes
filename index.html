HOW TO PLAY

  1. Take Turns Drawing Lines: Tap or click any open dashed line between two dots.
  2. Complete Boxes: The player who closes the 4th side of any 1×1 square claims that box and scores 1 point.
  3. Bonus Turns: Completing a box awards an immediate extra turn! Chain multiple boxes together to dominate the board.
  4. Win the Game: When every box on the board is claimed, the player with the most boxes wins!
  5. Play with a Friend: Click Host Private Match and share the invitation link or 5-letter code with your friend.
